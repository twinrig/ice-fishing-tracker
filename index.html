Tournament Setup

Overall is automatic – everyone is in it, so don't add it here. Kids are placed in the first youth division whose range contains their age (from their age or birth date) and also appear in Overall. Leave Min blank for "and under" (e.g. Max 12 = "12 & Under"). Changing ranges re-assigns everyone.

Big screen & header

The big screen shows the top 10 Overall, top 5 in each youth division and top 3 in each species, and scrolls through them: it pauses at the top, scrolls down at the speed below, pauses at the bottom, then starts over. The leaderboard page shows everyone.

Slow ≈ 20, normal ≈ 40, fast ≈ 80.

Import Participants (pre-registrations or walk-ups)

CSV columns: name, email, phone (both expected – rows missing them are flagged), age and/or a birth date (birthdate, birth_date, dob, date_of_birth, birthday; formats 2014-03-09, 3/9/2014, 03/09/2014, 3/9/14), optional hole_tickets (1 or 2, “one”/“two”). Ages are worked out as of the tournament's event date; if a row has both, the age column wins. Everyone is in Overall; kids are placed in their youth division automatically from their age. A division column, if present, is ignored. Participant numbers are always assigned automatically (an id column is ignored). Re-importing the same file won't create duplicates.
