Tournament Setup

Participants are placed in the first Age/Division whose range contains their age. Leave Min or Max blank for open-ended (e.g. Max 13 = "13 & under", Min 19 = "19 and up"). Changing ranges re-assigns everyone not set by hand.

Leaderboards

For Species and Age/Division boards: "Each" makes a separate tab per species or group; "All …, grouped on one board" makes one tab with a separately ranked section for each; "Any …, combined ranking" ranks everyone together on one tab. Order controls the big-screen rotation.

Import Participants (pre-registrations or walk-ups)

CSV columns: name, optional age and/or a birth date (birthdate, birth_date, dob, date_of_birth, birthday; formats 2014-03-09, 3/9/2014, 03/09/2014, 3/9/14), Age/Division, email, phone, id (yyyy_#####). Ages are worked out as of the tournament's event date; a birth date wins over an age. With an age, the Age/Division is assigned automatically; without one, the file's Age/Division is used as a hand-set choice. Numbers are assigned automatically when id is blank. Re-importing the same file won't create duplicates.
